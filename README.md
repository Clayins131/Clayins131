## What I care about

I like the part of data work where numbers stop being abstract and start changing what people do.

The interesting bottleneck is often not the model. It is the gap between what the data says, what the dashboard shows, and what reaches the room where decisions happen. I am trying to get good at closing that gap: with data pipelines, visualisation, lightweight tools, and enough financial literacy to understand the stakes.

Right now I am especially interested in:

- financial data, valuation, risk, and markets
- decision-oriented dashboards and data products
- applied ML systems that survive contact with real workflows
- data engineering that makes analysis reliable instead of theatrical
