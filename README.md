## What I care about

I enjoy using data to understand complex problems and support practical decisions.

I am less interested in research for its own sake than in what happens after the analysis: when operating data reveals a business issue, when a model makes risk more visible, or when a dashboard helps a team decide what to do next.

I care about clear assumptions, reliable data, simple communication, and taking responsibility for the final output.

Right now, I am especially interested in:

* financial data, valuation, risk, and portfolio analytics
* quantitative methods applied to real market and business problems
* decision-oriented dashboards and data products
* technology strategy and data-driven transformation
* applied machine learning in real operational environments
